Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. About this policy

Super Reaction (the app) is a reaction-training app made by kgbrussia (we, us). This policy explains what happens with your data when you use it.

In short: the app works offline, keeps your results on your device and does not use ads or analytics at the moment.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Your results, records, personal goals, favorites and history of attempts.
  • App settings: theme, language, number of rounds, sound and reminder options.

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with the reset buttons in the app, by clearing the app data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

The app does not currently include advertising or analytics tools. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

4. Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: used only for local reminders to train. They are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app settings or in Android settings.
  • Vibration: used by some exercises and for tap feedback.
  • Motion sensors: used by the shaking exercise to count shakes. Sensor readings are processed on the device and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Sound: some exercises play short sounds.

5. Contacting support

If you write to us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in the message. The support button may add the app version and your device model to the draft; you can delete them before sending. We use this information only to answer you and improve the app.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. The share button opens the standard Android share sheet; what you send and to whom is up to you.

7. Children

The app is a general training game and is not aimed at collecting any information from children. Because it stores only gameplay data on the device, no personal information is gathered.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
